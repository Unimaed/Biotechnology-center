ABOUT THE CENTRE

Introducing the Centre

The North East Zonal Biotechnology Centre of Excellence is located at the University of Maiduguri, Maiduguri, Borno State, Nigeria.

This section introduces the Centre, its establishment, activities and contribution to biotechnology research.

OUR DIRECTION

Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To develop the field of Biotechnology and related research activities in the University and to support the development of the vast bioresource potentials and need orientated Biotechnology research in the North-East Region.

Our Vision

The Centre aims to serve as a link and co-ordinating Centre for the various units of the University and the North-East Region in Biotechnology Research and Development.

RESEARCH FACILITIES

Molecular Biology Laboratory

The Centre provides molecular biology facilities and technical services to support scientific research, molecular investigations and advanced biotechnology.

Extraction, isolation and quantification of DNA and RNA for molecular biology research and analysis.

Conventional and Real-Time PCR

Molecular amplification and analysis using conventional PCR and real-time PCR techniques.

Gel Electrophoresis and Gene Cloning

Molecular separation, analysis and gene cloning techniques supporting biotechnology research.

Sequencing Services

Sequence-related services supporting molecular characterization and research investigations.
